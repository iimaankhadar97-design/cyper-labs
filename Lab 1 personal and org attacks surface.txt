Student Questions

6. Which asset is most critical?
Student Database, because it contains important student information such as registration, grades, and personal records. Loss or unauthorized access could seriously affect the university.

7. Which risk has the greatest impact?
Ransomware attack on the University Server has the greatest potential impact because it could make many university systems and data unavailable at the same time.

8. Which control is preventive and which is detective?

Preventive control: MFA  prevents unauthorized users from accessing accounts even if a password is stolen.
Detective control: Audit logs monitoring  detects and records suspicious or unauthorized activity after or while it occurs.
