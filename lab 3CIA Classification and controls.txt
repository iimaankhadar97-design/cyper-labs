Student Questions

20. Can one incident affect all three CIA properties?

Yes. One incident can affect Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability at the same time. For example, a ransomware attack can expose confidential data, modify or encrypt files, and make systems unavailable.

21. Why is backup primarily an availability/recovery control?

Backup helps restore lost or damaged data and systems after an incident, allowing users to access them again. Therefore, backup is primarily an Availability and Recovery control.
